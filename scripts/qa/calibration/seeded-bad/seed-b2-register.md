Type 7 Ne-Si Mobilizer — Generative Momentum
SEED: B2 register drift — exclamation marks, coaching imperatives, fortune-telling. Must pass Stage A.

**Core Motivation:**
The drive is toward expansion — more options, more possibilities, more life in the space of a life. The Ne function makes this architectural: you don't experience possibility as abstract, you experience it as urgent and concrete, each new idea pulling attention toward it with genuine force. The Si undertow is the friction: memory, precedent, and the accumulated weight of past experiences that didn't go as planned. Start paying attention to how often the excitement about what's next is also an escape from what didn't quite work before. When you do, you will find the pattern clarifies immediately! The Ne-Si tension is where your most honest self-understanding lives. Embrace this insight — it's where the real work begins.

**Processing Architecture:**
Ne generates possibilities laterally and fast. You move from one connection to the next with speed that can look effortless from the outside but requires significant ongoing management — not all possibilities are worth pursuing, and the architecture doesn't naturally filter for quality at the intake stage. Si provides the comparative database: you've seen versions of this before, you know what worked and what didn't, and that knowledge is stored in a way that can interrupt the Ne flow with uncomfortable relevance. Make sure you build in regular check-ins with that Si database! You will find that slowing down to consult past experience — rather than generating forward — dramatically improves your decision-making. Start treating your accumulated knowledge as an asset rather than a constraint.

**Contribution and Drain:**
What you bring to any room is generative energy that makes others more creative! You don't just have ideas; you produce them in quantities that shift the temperature of the conversation. The Si function means you also carry institutional memory, even when it feels irrelevant — you know how versions of this were tried before, and you're one of the people most likely to flag the historical context before the group commits to something it has already failed at. Embrace this dual role: future-facing energy and past-informing wisdom together make you indispensable. The drain is environments where the ideas are unwelcome — where the culture is too rigid to absorb what you generate. Don't tolerate those environments! You deserve better.

**Intersection:**
The Heart, Head, and Hand combine here in ways that are genuinely energizing when properly channeled. Your Heart drive to experience positive affect and avoid pain finds its outlet in the Ne's capacity to generate alternative framings — when something is painful, there's always another way to look at it. Start using this more intentionally! You will find that the Ne reframing capacity, applied consciously, dramatically reduces the drag from the Si accumulation of what didn't work. The intersection of these three centers is your most powerful operating point. Learn to access it deliberately rather than waiting for it to happen. Practice entering it on purpose, not just when momentum carries you there naturally.

**Blind Spot:**
The gap hardest to see is that the reframing which feels like resilience is sometimes avoidance. The Ne can always find another angle, which means that sitting with a concluded failure and fully integrating it — the Si's job — gets interrupted before it's complete. You move on faster than the experience is processed, which means the Si archive fills with half-digested events that activate at unexpected moments. Stop running from the closed loop! Begin building a practice of deliberate closure. You will find it enormously freeing — the creative energy that's been tied up managing incomplete experiences will release back into the present. This is the shift that unlocks the next phase of your growth.

**In the Room:**
The active signature is ideational contagion. You are one of the most energizing people to work with when the environment matches your mode — your enthusiasm is genuine, your connections are often useful, and your ability to keep the energy high through difficulty is not nothing. It's a real contribution! What's less visible from inside the experience is how much cognitive and social management others are doing to work with your pace. Make a practice of slowing down and checking in: not as a performance of care, but as a genuine question about whether others are keeping up or being outpaced. You will find the collaboration improves significantly. Learn to make room for others' processing speed.

**Growth Edge:**
The leverage point is the Si function — specifically, the honest conversation with your own history rather than the strategic reframe of it. The Ne will always generate a new angle. The growth edge is choosing, sometimes, to stay with the old one long enough for it to finish teaching you. This sounds like slowing down, but it's actually the thing that makes the Ne more accurate over time: a Ne that is well-fed by an integrated Si produces better possibilities, not just more of them. Start here. Practice staying with one completed experience until you understand what it actually contained rather than what you wished it contained. You will discover resources you didn't know you were carrying.

**What Would Disconfirm:**
You would question this profile if you found that possibility doesn't generate urgency — that you can hold many options without feeling pulled by any of them, or that you're comfortable with a single trajectory over an extended period without experiencing it as constriction. You would also question it if you discovered that past experience is your primary orienting frame rather than a constraint to work around, or that the future feels less compelling than the present moment. A person who is energized by deepening rather than broadening, or who finds the accumulation of established precedent more satisfying than the generation of new options, probably has a different cognitive architecture. The pressure of possibility is the diagnostic here.
